## How to Sync Your Pocket Articles to Folio

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
  Your Pocket saves are being imported in the background. This may take some time depending on how many articles you've saved. Feel free to explore Folio or even close the app—your articles will gradually appear as the import completes.
</Callout>

The process is simple: 

1. Click on the `⚙️ Settings` icon in the Bottom-nav bar of the Folio app.
2. Select the `Import` option.
3. Click the `Pocket (Sign In)` option.
4. Click `Start Import` to be redirected to Pocket Sign In (if you aren't already logged in).
5. Select the `Authorize` option.
6. Your import will begin and you can close the Pocket page.
