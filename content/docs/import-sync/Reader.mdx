## Export from Readwise Reader

To export your saved content from Readwise Reader, follow these steps:
1. Open the [Readwise Reader app](https://readwise.io/dashboard).
2. Click on the [`Export`](https://readwise.io/export) option or use this direct link: https://readwise.io/export
3. Scroll down to the `CSV` section and click `Export`.

![Readwise_Import.png](/import/Readwise_Import.png)

## Import to Folio

Once you've downloaded the Readwise Reader CSV file, follow these steps to import your bookmarks into Fol
io:
1. Click the [**`⚙️ Settings`**](https://app.savewithfolio.com/settings) icon in the Folio app's bottom navigation bar.
2. Select the [**`Import`**](https://app.savewithfolio.com/import) option from the Settings page.
3. Choose **`Readwise Reader (.CSV)`**.
4. Folio will open a file explorer where you can upload your **`Readwise Reader CSV`** file.
5. Confirm that you're uploading the correct Instapaper CSV file.
6. Wait for the import process to complete.
7. Check the success message when the import process finishes.

![Import-to-Folio-Reader.png](/import/Import-to-Folio-Reader.png)

## ⁉️ FAQs

<Accordions>
    <Accordion title="🕓 How long does an import take?">
        - Small files (under 500 items) → usually a few seconds.
        - Larger imports → may take a few minutes; Folio will send you a notification when done.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="❌ My Readwise Reader file won’t upload into Folio. What should I do?">
        - <p> Make sure it’s the **CSV export file**. </p>
        - Check that the file is not too large (split into multiple exports if needed).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="💡 How can I verify whether my Readwise Reader data was successfully imported into Folio?">
        - Folio will display a confirmation message indicating whether your data was successfully imported into your account.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="⬇️ Should I keep the app open during the Readwise Reader CSV import process?">
        - Yes, you need to keep the app open until the import process is completed and you see the success message.
    </Accordion>
        <Accordion title="🎨 Does Folio import customized saves (such as Favorites, Highlights, and Tags) from Readwise Reader?">
        - Yes, all customized saves will display in their correct tabs (**Favorites, Highlights, Tags, and Archived**) on Folio
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="🧩 Is there another way to import from Readwise Reader to Folio?">
        - Not yet! However, we're working on integrating with Readwise Reader's API so you can import your saves with a single click 🚀!
    </Accordion>
</Accordions>
