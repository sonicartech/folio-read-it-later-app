## Export from Raindrop.io

To export your content from Raindrop as a CSV file:
1. Navigate to the [Raindrop app](https://app.raindrop.io/my/0).
2. Click your email address at the top left of the Raindrop sidebar.
3. Select the [`⚙️ Settings`](https://app.raindrop.io/settings/app) option.
4. Click on [`Backups`](https://app.raindrop.io/settings/backups).
5. Select `➕ Create New`.
6. Wait for the app to prepare your backup.
7. Refresh the page to view the new backup.
8. Click `CSV` to download the file.

![import-Raindrop-Import](/import/Raindrop_Import.png)

## Import to Folio

Once you've downloaded the Raindrop CSV file, follow these steps to import your bookmarks into Folio:
1. Click the [**`⚙️ Settings`**](https://app.savewithfolio.com/settings) icon in the Folio app's bottom navigation bar.
2. Select the [**`Import`**](https://app.savewithfolio.com/import) option from the Settings page.
3. Choose **`Raindrop.io (.CSV)`**.
4. Folio will open a file explorer where you can upload your **`Raindrop.io CSV`** file.
5. Confirm that you're uploading the correct Instapaper CSV file.
6. Wait for the import process to complete.
7. Check the success message when the import process finishes.

## ⁉️ FAQs

<Accordions>
    <Accordion title="🕓 How long does an import take?">
        - Small files (under 500 items) → usually a few seconds.
        - Larger imports → may take a few minutes; Folio will send you a notification when done.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="❌ My Raindrop file won’t upload into Folio. What should I do?">
        - Make sure it’s the **CSV export file**.
        - Check that the file is not too large (split into multiple exports if needed).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="💡 How can I verify whether my Raindrop data was successfully imported into Folio?">
        - Folio will display a confirmation message indicating whether your data was successfully imported into your account.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="⬇️ Should I keep the app open during the Raindrop CSV import process?">
        - Yes, you need to keep the app open until the import process is completed and you see the success message.
    </Accordion>
        <Accordion title="🎨 Does Folio import customized saves (such as Favorites, Highlights, and Tags) from Raindrop?">
        - Yes, all customized saves will display in their correct tabs (**Favorites, Highlights, Tags, and Archived**) on Folio
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="🧩 Is there another way to import from Raindrop to Folio?">
        - Not yet! However, we're working on integrating with Raindrop's API so you can import your saves with a single click 🚀!
    </Accordion>
</Accordions>
