## Managing Your Account

To manage your account:

1. Click the ⚙️ settings icon in the bottom navigation bar of the app.
2. Select `Manage Account` from the Settings page.
3. You can now view and update your account information.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
    Ensure to save any changes you make to your account information before exiting the page.
</Callout>

## ⁉️ FAQs

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="How do I update my email address?">
    To update your email address, navigate to the `Manage Account` section in the settings, click on your current email, enter the new email address, and save the changes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How can I change my password?">
    To change your password, go to the `Manage Account` section in the settings, select the `Change Password` option, enter your current password followed by the new password, and confirm the change.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What should I do if I forget my password?">
    If you forget your password, you can [reset it](reset-my-password.mdx) by clicking on the `Forgot Password` link on the login page. Follow the instructions sent to your registered email to create a new password.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
