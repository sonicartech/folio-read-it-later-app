## 🔐 How to Reset Your Password

To reset your password:

1. Log out of your account.
2. Click the `Sign In` option.
3. Select `Forgot Password`.
4. Enter your registered email address to receive a password reset link.
5. Check your inbox or spam folder for the email.
6. Click on the reset link in the email.
7. Enter and confirm your new password.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
    If you do not receive the password reset email within a few minutes, please check your spam or junk folder. If you still cannot find it, ensure that you entered the correct email address associated with your account.
</Callout>

## ⁉️ FAQs

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="What should I do if I don't receive the password reset email?">
    If you don't receive the password reset email, please check your spam or junk folder. Ensure that you entered the correct email address associated with your account. If you still cannot find it, try requesting the reset link again or contact support for assistance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use a previously used password when resetting my password?">
    This depends on the security policies of Folio. Some systems may restrict the reuse of recent passwords. It's recommended to choose a new, strong password that you haven't used before.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long is the password reset link valid?">
    The validity period of the password reset link can vary. Typically, it is valid for a limited time (4 hours). Please refer to the instructions in the email for specific details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What should I do if I forget my new password after resetting it?">
    If you forget your new password, you can repeat the password reset process by clicking on the `Forgot Password` link on the login page and following the instructions to create a new password.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the app validate my password length and complexity?">
    Yes, you need to enter a strong password to proceed. General and common passwords are not acceptable.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
