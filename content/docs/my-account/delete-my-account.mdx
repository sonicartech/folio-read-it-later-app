## How to Delete Your Account

To delete your account:

1. Click the ⚙️ settings icon in the bottom navigation bar of the app.
2. Select `Manage Account` from the Settings page.
3. Tap the `Delete Account` option.
4. Confirm your action.

<Callout type="warning" title="Warning">
    Deleting your account is irreversible. All your data will be permanently removed and cannot be recovered. Please ensure you have backed up any important information before proceeding with account deletion.
</Callout>

## ⁉️ FAQs

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Can I recover my account after deletion?">
    No, once you delete your account, all your data is permanently removed and cannot be recovered.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will my data be deleted from all devices?">
    Yes, deleting your account will remove your data from all devices where you have used Folio.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to my subscriptions or purchases?">
    Any active subscriptions or purchases associated with your account will be canceled upon deletion. Please contact support for more details.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
