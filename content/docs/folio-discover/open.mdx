## 🧭 How to Open Folio Discover Items in Your Browser

With Folio Discover, you can view the original content by opening it in your browser. To do this:

1. Select an item from Discover.
2. Click `...` (3-dot icon) at the bottom-right.
3. Select the `Open in Browser` option.

## ⁉️ FAQs

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I click 'Open in Browser'?">
    Clicking `Open in Browser` will open the original source of the content in a new browser tab or window, allowing you to view it directly from its original location.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I open multiple items in the browser at once?">
    No, you need to open each item individually by selecting it and clicking 'Open in Browser'.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if the link doesn't work?">
    If the link doesn't work, ensure that you have an active internet connection and that the original source is still available. If issues persist, contact support for assistance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I read an item directly in Folio?">
    Yes, you can add items from Discover to Reader by clicking the `➕` icon next to any selected item.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
