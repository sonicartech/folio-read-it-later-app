## 🔗 How to Share Content from Folio Discover

You can share any item from Folio Discover with other users:

1. Select an item from Discover.
2. Click `...` (3-dot icon) at the bottom-right.
3. Select the `Share` option.

![Share from Folio Discover](/discover/Share.png)

## 📤 Sharing Options

When you select the `Share` option, you will see several sharing methods:
- **Copy Link**: Copies the article link to your clipboard for easy sharing.
- **Share via `...`**: Opens the native sharing options on your device, allowing you to share the link through various apps and services.
