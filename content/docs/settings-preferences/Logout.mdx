## How to Sign Out from Folio

To sign out from your Folio account:
1. Click on the `⚙️` Settings icon in the bottom navigation bar of the Folio app.
2. Select the `Logout` option.
3. Confirm your logout action.
4. You'll be redirected to the Sign Up page.
