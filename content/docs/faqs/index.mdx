## ⁉️ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

<Accordions type="single">

  <Accordion title="📖 What is the Folio app and what does it provide?">
    - Folio is a modern **read-it-later** app with powerful features that helps you discover the latest news and feeds through its simple Discover interface. It allows you to sort, organize, and customize your saved content easily. Folio is available on iOS, Android, iPad, Web, and also provides browser extensions for one-click saving.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="📱 Which Operating Systems Does Folio Support?">
    Folio is available on multiple platforms to ensure seamless access across devices.  
    - Google Play Store for Android and Tablets → https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.lessisbetter.folio  
    - Apple Store for iOS and iPads → https://apps.apple.com/eg/app/folio-save-now-read-later/id6746292066  
    - Folio on Web → https://app.savewithfolio.com/  
    - Browser Extensions:  
      - **Chrome** → [Extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/dodpedlanihpjnhfmacllipmamhbkfap?utm_source=item-share-cb)  
      - **Firefox** → [Extension](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/folio-save-now-read-later/)  
      - **MS Edge** → [Extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/dodpedlanihpjnhfmacllipmamhbkfap?utm_source=item-share-cb)  
      - **Safari** → [Extension](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/save-to-folio/id6749776863)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="🔒 Is my data secure with Folio?">
    - Yes, Folio takes your privacy and data security seriously. All your saved content is stored securely, and your data is never shared with third parties without your consent. For more details, you can review our <a href="https://savewithfolio.com/legal/privacy/">Privacy Policy</a>.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="💾 How do I save content to Folio?">
    - You can save content to Folio in several convenient ways. On mobile, use the "Share Picker" and select Folio. On desktop, install the 🧩 browser extension for one-click saving. You can also paste URLs directly into the app or use the built-in share feature.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="🗂️ How can I organize my saved content?">
    - Folio makes it easy to stay organized. Your saved items are automatically grouped into tabs like All, Favorites, Tagged, Highlights, Short Reads, Long Reads, and Archived. You can also create custom tags and rely on the powerful search feature to quickly find what you need.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="🔍 Can I search within my saved articles?">
    - Yes, Folio includes a [powerful search feature](../folio-reader/Search-on-Your-Saves.mdx) that lets you search not only by titles, URLs, and tags but also within the full text of your saved articles for easy retrieval.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="🌐 Does Folio work offline?">
    - Absolutely. Once articles are saved to Folio, you can access and read them without an internet connection, making it ideal for offline reading.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="💡 What is Folio Discover?">
    - [Folio Discover](../folio-discover/index.mdx) is an integrated feature that helps you explore new content tailored to your interests. It curates fresh articles and stories from a variety of sources, keeping your reading list constantly updated.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="⚙️ How do I customize my Folio experience?">
    - Folio allows you to personalize your reading experience through settings such as theme (light or dark mode), font size, and notification preferences. These options give you control to tailor the app to your liking.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="📧 How can I contact Folio support?">
    - If you need help or have questions, you can contact Folio support directly through the app or by visiting our <a href="https://support.savewithfolio.com/en/">Support Page</a>. Our team is here to assist you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="📝 Where can I find more information about Folio?">
    - For more in-depth details about Folio’s features, updates, and usage guides, visit our <a href="https://docs.savewithfolio.com/">Documentation</a> or check out the <a href="https://savewithfolio.com/blog/">Blog</a>.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="🔁 Can I import and migrate my saved content from other apps to Folio?">
    - Yes, Folio supports importing your saved content from other read-it-later and bookmarking services, including **[Pocket](../import-sync/index.mdx), [Instapaper](../import-sync/Instapaper.mdx), [Matter](../import-sync/Matter.mdx), [Raindrop.io](../import-sync/Raindrop.mdx), and [Readwise Reader](../import-sync/Reader.mdx)**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="📖 What is Folio Reader?">
    - [Folio Reader](../folio-reader/index.mdx) is the core module that organizes and manages your saved content. It provides tabs like All, Favorites, Tagged, Highlights, Short Reads, Long Reads, and Archived, helping you easily navigate and revisit your collection.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="🎧 Can I listen to my saves offline?">
    - Yes, you can [listen to your saved articles offline](../folio-reader/listen-to-your-saves.mdx) as long as the content has been downloaded and is available in your Folio Reader.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="⏩ Can I adjust the playback speed while listening?">
    - Folio Reader supports adjustable playback speed ranging from 0.5X to 2X, allowing you to fine-tune your listening experience.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="🎵 Can I listen to my saves while using other apps?">
    - Yes, audio playback continues in the background, meaning you can listen to your saves while using other apps on your device.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="📙 How can I find display settings?">
    - To access [display settings](../folio-reader/display-settings.mdx), open any item in your Folio Reader, tap the `...` (three-dot icon) in the top-right corner of your article, and then select **Display Settings** from the menu.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="🔂 Are my display settings synced across all my devices?">
    - Yes, all of your chosen display configurations are automatically synced across every device where you use Folio, ensuring a consistent reading experience.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="↩️ Can I reset my display settings to default?">
    - Yes, you can reset all customizations back to default by tapping the ↩️ reset option at the top-right of the Display Settings popup screen.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
