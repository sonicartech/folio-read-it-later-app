## 🔍 How to Search Through Your Saves
To search for any content within your Folio Reader saves:

1. Click on the `🔍` icon in the header (top right) of Folio.
2. A `🔍 Search Bar` will appear where you can type your query.
3. Real-time search results will display as you type.
4. Click on any result to view the content.
5. Enjoy reading what you've been looking for!
