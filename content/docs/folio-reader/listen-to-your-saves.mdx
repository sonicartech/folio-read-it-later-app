## 🎵 How to Listen to My Saves?

Listen to Your Saves feature is available in Folio Reader. To listen to any saved content: **Open your item → Click the "🎧" icon at the top-right → Enjoy listening!**

![Listen to Your Saves](/reader/listen/Listen.png)

## 🎶 Playback Controls

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
  Folio Reader provides playback controls while listening to your selected save, allowing you to Play & Pause and adjust Speed (0.5X, 0.75X, 1X, 1.25X, 1.5X, and 2X).
</Callout>

### ⏯️ Play & Pause

While listening to your save, the playback controls remain visible at the bottom, allowing you to read or navigate to any page while listening.

The `Play button (▶️)` starts audio playback of saved content, while the `Pause button (⏸️)` temporarily stops the audio without closing the playback session, allowing users to resume listening from the same position later.

![Play & Pause](/reader/listen/Play_Pause.png)

### ⏩ Playback Speed

The playback speed feature lets you adjust how quickly audio content plays. Multiple speed options (`0.5X, 0.75X, 1X, 1.25X, 1.5X, and 2X`) are available, allowing you to customize your listening experience based on your personal preference.

![Playback Speed](/reader/listen/Speed.png)

## ⁉️ FAQs

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Can I listen to my saves offline?">
    Yes, you can listen to your saves offline as long as the content has been downloaded and is available in your Folio Reader.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I adjust the playback speed while listening?">
    Yes, Folio Reader allows you to adjust the playback speed with options ranging from 0.5X to 2X, enabling you to customize your listening experience.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I listen to my saves while using other apps?">
    Yes, you can listen to your saves in the background while using other apps on your device. The audio will continue playing even if you switch to a different app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I end or close my listening session?">
    Simply click the "❌" icon at the bottom right of the playback controls to end your listening session.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
