## 🔤 Choose Custom Fonts and Sizes

### 🔠 Available Reader Fonts

Choose your preferred font from these options:

- **Literata:** A modern serif font designed for comfortable long reading, often used in eBooks.
- **Lora:** A balanced serif with smooth curves, good for both screen and print.
- **Alegreya:** A dynamic serif with a calligraphic feel, giving text a lively and human touch.
- **Spectral:** A versatile serif with a contemporary look, optimized for reading on digital devices.
- **Vollkorn:** A strong, classic serif that feels traditional yet readable for long passages.

![Available Reader Fonts](/reader/display-settings/Available_Reader_Fonts.png)

### 🔭 Customize Reader Font Size

You can adjust the text size for comfortable reading:

- **Small:** Fits more text on the screen.
- **Medium:** Balanced size for most readers.
- **Large:** Easier on the eyes, less text per page.
- **Extra Large:** Maximum readability with the largest text.

![ Customize Reader Font Size.png](/reader/display-settings/Customize_Reader_Font_Size.png)

## 〰️ Control Typography Settings

Adjust line spacing to match your reading comfort:

- **Compact:** Tight spacing, more text on screen.
- **Normal:** Standard spacing for balanced reading.
- **Relaxed:** Extra breathing room between lines.
- **Loose:** Widest spacing for maximum readability.

![Control Typography Settings.png](/reader/display-settings/Control_Typography_Settings.png)

## 📖 Adjust Reading Spacing

Control the margins around your text:

- **Narrow:** Minimal margins, more words per line.
- **Medium:** Balanced margins for easy reading.
- **Wide:** Extra spacing at the sides for comfort.
- **Full:** Maximum margins, highly focused reading view.

![Advanced Reader Customization.png](/reader/display-settings/Advanced_Reader_Customization.png)

## 🖥️ Configure Display Options

Choose how text is aligned on the page:

- **Align Left:** Straight left edge, ragged right edge.
- **Justify:** Straight edges on both sides for a clean, block-style look.

![Configure Display Options.png](/reader/display-settings/Configure_Display_Options.png)

## ⁉️ FAQs

<Accordions type="single">

  <Accordion title="📙 How can I find display settings?">
    - To access display settings, open any item in your Folio Reader, tap the `...` (three-dot icon) in the top-right corner of your article, and then select **Display Settings** from the menu.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="❓ Are these display settings synced across my devices?">
    - Yes. Display settings are stored in your Folio account and sync automatically across all devices for a consistent reading experience.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="🎨 Can I use custom fonts not listed in the options?">
    - Currently, Folio Reader supports only the built-in font options. More fonts may be added in future updates based on user feedback.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="🔧 What should I do if display settings are not applying correctly?">
    - If your display settings don’t seem to apply, try restarting the app or logging out and back into your Folio account. If issues persist, please contact Folio support for assistance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="💡 Can I reset display settings to default?">
    - Yes, you can reset all customizations to default by tapping the `↩️` reset option at the top-right of the Display Settings popup screen.
  </Accordion>

</Accordions>
