## 🗑️ Add to Archive

To archive any item:

- Select an item from any tab.
- Click the `...` (3-dot icon).
- Select the `Archive` option.

![Add to Archive](/reader/archive/Archive_Item.png)

## 🔁 Remove from Archive

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
  Removing an item from the archive will restore it to its original tab (All, Favorites, or Highlights).
</Callout>

To restore an item from the archive:
- Navigate to the `Archived` tab.
- Select the item you wish to restore.
- Click the `...` (3-dot icon).
- Choose the `Unarchive` option.

![Remove from Archive](/reader/archive/Unarchive_Item.png)

## ⁉️ FAQs

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Will archiving an item delete it from my device?">
    No, archiving an item does not delete it from your device. It simply moves the item to the Archive tab, keeping your main tabs clean and organized.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I still access my highlights and tags after archiving an item?">
    Yes, all your highlights, tags, and notes will be preserved when you archive an item. You can access them anytime by unarchiving the item.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a limit to how many items I can archive?">
    No, there is no limit to the number of items you can archive. Feel free to archive as many items as you need to keep your Reader organized.
  </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I search for archived items?">
    Yes, you can search for archived items using the search functionality within the Archive tab.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Will archiving an item affect its sync status across devices?">
    No, archiving an item will not affect its sync status. Your archived items will still sync across all your devices as long as you are logged into the same account.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I delete items from the Archive?">
    **Yes**. **Please note that this action permanently removes your save from Folio and cannot be undone.**
    To delete an item:
        - Select your item → Click `...` (3-dot icon) → Click "`🗑️ Delete`".
    </Accordion>
</Accordions>
