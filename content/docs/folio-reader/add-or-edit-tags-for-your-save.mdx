## Adding New Tag(s)
Creating new tags is simple:

1. Click `...` (3-dot icon) on your selected item.
2. Select `Edit Tags` option.
3. Add your new tag(s), then click `Done`.

[](https://www.notion.so)
## Viewing Saves by Tag(s)
To view your tagged items, simply click on the `🏷️ Tagged` tab and the app will display all your tags for you to select from and view your saves accordingly.

[](https://www.notion.so)
## ⁉️ FAQs
<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="🏷️ Can I add tags from any tab in the Folio Reader?">
   - Yes, you can add new tags in all Folio Reader tabs. Simply select your item, click the `...` (3-dot icon), type your new tag(s), and click Done to save!
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="✅ Can I select multiple tags to view my saves?">
   - Yes, you can select multiple tags to filter your saves. Simply use the Tagged tab in your Reader to select and view items organized by your chosen tag combinations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="🔁 **Are tags synced across my devices?**">
   - Yes. Tags are stored in your Folio account and sync automatically across all devices.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="🔍 **Can I search by tags?**">
   - Yes. Use the **Search bar** and filter by tag to quickly find articles.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="🗑️ **Can I delete or edit my tags?**">
   - Yes, you can delete or edit your tags. Simply select the tag you want to modify, then click on it to either delete or update it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="🏷️ **What happens if I delete my tags?**">
   - When you delete your tags, your saved items will automatically revert to the default tags assigned by the source. These items will then appear under the All tab in your Folio Reader and be removed from the Tagged tab.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
