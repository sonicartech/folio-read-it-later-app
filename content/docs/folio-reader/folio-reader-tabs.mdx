## ✨ All Tab

The `All` tab is the default location for newly saved items. This tab displays all your saved content except for archived items. Items are automatically sorted with the newest appearing first. When you first open Folio Reader, the `All` tab is the default view.

### How to Organize Saves in the `All` tab

The `All` tab provides several organization options to keep your content neat and organized. Access these features by tapping the `...` (3-dot icon):

1. **Add To Favorite**
2. **Archive**
3. **Use Tags**
4. **Delete**

![All Tab](/reader/reader-tabs/All-Tab.png)

## ⭐ Favorites Tab

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
Your Favorites tab contains all your favorite items, even those you've archived.
</Callout>

This tab displays all your saves marked as favorites, allowing quick access to them in one place. After reading, you can unfavorite saves or move them to the archive.

There are two ways to **add an item to your Favorites tab**:

- Click the “🌟” icon at the bottom right of your selected item.
- Click the `...` (3-dot icon) → then select the `⭐ Favorite` option.

There are two ways to **remove an item from the Favorites tab**:

- Open the Favorites tab → Click the "⭐" icon again to remove the item.
- Open the Favorites tab → Click the `...` (3-dot icon) → Select the `⭐ Unfavorite` option.

![Favorites Tab](/reader/reader-tabs/Favorites-Tab.png)

## 🏷️ Tagged Tab

Folio's tagging feature allows you to customize your saves by creating custom tags and easily access your content by selecting the tags you've created.

### 🏷️ How to Create Tags for Your Saves

To create new custom tags for your selected save:

1. Click on the `...` icon for the selected item.
2. Select the `🏷️ Edit Tags` option.
3. Now you can either create a new tag or select from your existing tags.

![Create Tags](/reader/reader-tabs/Create-Tags.png)

### 👀 How to View Saves with Your Custom Tags

Simply click on the `🏷️ Tagged` tab and Folio will display a list of tags by default. Select any tag(s) to view the corresponding saved items.

![Tagged Tab](/reader/reader-tabs/Tagged-Tab.png)

## ⚡ Highlights Tab

Under the `Highlights` tab, you can find all your highlighted saves displayed in one place for easy access. When opening any highlighted save, the app will automatically show the first highlighted content, allowing you to continue from where you left off while reading.

### How to Highlight Saves?

1. Open any saved article.
2. Select the content you want to highlight.
3. Click the “`Highlight`" button (appears automatically after selecting content).

### How to Remove Highlights?

1. Open any highlighted article from the "Highlights" tab.
2. Select any highlighted content (yellow).
3. Click the "`Remove Highlight`" button (appears automatically after selecting content).

![Highlights Tab](/reader/reader-tabs/Highlights-Tab.png)

## 🕑 Short Reads Tab

The `Short Reads` tab displays all articles that take **less than 10 minutes to read**, except those in the Archive tab. This feature helps you quickly find content when you have limited time. You can organize items in this tab by tapping the `...` (3-dot icon), which offers these options:

1. **Add To Favorite**
2. **Edit Tags**
3. **Archive**
4. **Delete**

![Short Reads Tab](/reader/reader-tabs/Short_Reads-Tab.png)

## 🕙 Long Reads Tab

The `Long Reads` tab displays all articles that take **more than 10 minutes to read**, except those in the Archive tab. This feature helps you find longer content when you have more time available. You can organize items in this tab by tapping the `...` (3-dot icon), which offers these options:

1. **Add To Favorite**
2. **Edit Tags**
3. **Archive**
4. **Delete**

![Long Reads Tab](/reader/reader-tabs/Long_Reads-Tab.png)

## 🗑️ Archived Tab

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
Under the `Archived` tab, all your **highlighted and tagged saves remain stored** so when you **unarchive** them, **every customization will be restored to its related tab 💯**.
</Callout>

The `Archived` tab keeps your Reader clean and organized by storing articles you've marked as `Archived`.

### How to Archive Saves?

To archive any item:

- Select an item from any tab → Click the `...` (3-dot icon) → Select the `Archive` option.

### How to Unarchive Saves?

To unarchive any archived item:

- Open the `Archived` tab → Select any item → Click the `...` (3-dot icon) → Select the `Unarchive` option.

![Archived Tab](/reader/reader-tabs/Archived-Tab.png)

## ⁉️ FAQs

<Accordions>
    <Accordion title="How do I switch between different Reader tabs?">
        To switch between different Reader tabs, simply tap on the desired tab at the top of the screen. Each tab is labeled with its respective name (e.g., All, Favorites, Tagged, Highlights, Short Reads, Long Reads, Archived).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I sort and organize my items in different Reader tabs?">
        Yes, we provide a `Sort By` feature in all Reader tabs:        
            - **Newest First**: Displays your saves with the most recently added items at the top.
            - **Oldest First**: Displays your saves with the earliest added items at the top.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="How can I delete my saved articles?">
        **Note that this action permanently removes your save from Folio and cannot be restored.**
        To delete any item(s):
            - Select your item → Click `...` (3-dot icon) → Click "`🗑️ Delete`".
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I unarchive my archived saves?">
        Yes, you can unarchive items from the Archived tab:
            - Select your item → Click `...` (3-dot icon) → Click "**Unarchive**".
    </Accordion>
</Accordions>
