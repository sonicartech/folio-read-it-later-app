## 📖 What Is Folio Reader?

Folio Reader is the core feature of Folio which allows you to manage and organize your saves. You can customize your content by marking saves as favorites, adding highlights, categorizing them as short or long reads, and moving items to archive.

![Folio Reader](/reader/home/Folio_Reader.png)
## 🎲 Overview of Reader Tabs

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
  Folio Reader covers seven main tabs (**All**, **Favorites**, **Tagged**, **Highlights**, **Short Reads**, **Long Reads**, and **Archive**). Each tab is designed to help you organize and manage your saves effectively.
</Callout>

### ✨ All Tab

In Folio Reader, the **`All`** tab is the default view when opening the Reader. It displays all your saves except those that have been archived. You can sort items in this tab by either "Newest First" or "Oldest First".

![All Tab](/reader/home/All-Tab.png)

### ⭐ Favorites Tab

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
  Your Favorites tab contains all your favorite items, even those you've archived.
</Callout>


This tab displays all your saves marked as favorites, allowing quick access to them in one place. After reading, you can unfavorite saves or move them to the archive.

To add an article to your favorites tab, click the "⭐" icon at the bottom right of your selected item.

![Favorites Tab](/reader/home/Favorites-Tab.png)

### 🏷️ Tagged Tab

You can customize your saves with the powerful tagging feature, which allows you to add custom tags to your content. When you create a new tag for your saves, they will be automatically added to the "Tagged" tab. 

To create a new tag(s) for your selected save:

- Select your item → Click `...` (3 dots icon) → Click "**Edit Tags**" → Add “🏷️ **Enter a Tag Name**” → Click "Save".

![Tagged Tab](/reader/home/Tagged-Tab.png)

### ⚡ Highlights Tab

This tab displays your highlighted saves, allowing you to access your highlights quickly. After reading, you can remove your highlights or move them to the archive.

To highlight an article for the Highlights tab:

- Simply **select content** from an article → Click the "**Highlight**" button that automatically appears → Your highlighted article will be added automatically to the "**Highlights**" tab.

![Highlights Tab](/reader/home/Highlights-Tab.png)

### 🕑 Short Reads Tab

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
  The Short Reads tab automatically displays all short saves (less than 10 minutes to read) except those in the Archive tab.
</Callout>

The Short Reads tab automatically displays articles that take **less than 10 minutes to read**, allowing you to quickly find content when you have limited time.

![Short Reads Tab](/reader/home/Short_Reads-Tab.png)

### 🕙 Long Reads Tab

---

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
  The Long Reads tab automatically displays all long saves (more than 10 minutes to read) except those in the Archive tab. 
</Callout>

The Long Reads tab automatically displays articles that take **more than 10 minutes to read**, allowing you to save longer content for when you have more time available.

![Long Reads Tab](/reader/home/Long_Reads-Tab.png)

### 🗑️ Archived Tab

In Folio Reader, we provide the Archive tab to help you store all your read content and keep the Reader more organized and clean. This allows you to enjoy reading and easily archive items when you're done 😃!

To archive your read saves:

- Select your item → Click `...` (3-dot icon) → Click "🗑️ **Archive**".

![Archived Tab](/reader/home/Archived-Tab.png)

## 🔡 Sort By (Available in All Tabs)

Folio Reader offers a **Sort By** feature in all Reader tabs, enabling you to organize your content by:

- **Newest First:** Displays your saves with the most recently added items at the top.
- **Oldest First:** Displays your saves with the earliest added items at the top.

![Sort By](/reader/home/Sort_By.png)
