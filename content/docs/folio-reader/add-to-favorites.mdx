## 🌟 Add To Favorites

There are two ways to **add an item to your Favorites tab**:

- Click the “🌟” icon at the bottom right of your selected item.
- Click the `...` (3-dot icon) → then select the `⭐ Favorite` option.

![Add To Favorites](/reader/favorites/Add_To_Favorites.png)

## ⭐ Remove From Favorites

There are two ways to **remove an item from the Favorites tab**:

- Open the Favorites tab → Click the "⭐" icon again to remove the item.
- Open the Favorites tab → Click the `...` (3-dot icon) → Select the `⭐ Unfavorite` option.

![Remove From Favorites](/reader/favorites/Remove_From_Favorites.png)

## ⁉️ FAQs

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Can I favorite an item from the All tab?">
    Yes, you can favorite any item from the All tab by clicking the "🌟" icon or using the 3-dot menu to select "⭐ Favorite".
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will favoriting an item move it out of the All tab?">
    No, favoriting an item does not remove it from the All tab. It will still be accessible there as well as in the Favorites tab.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I favorite archived items?">
    Yes, you can favorite archived items, and they will appear in your Favorites tab even if they are archived.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I know if an item is already favorited?">
    Favorited items will display a filled "🌟" icon, indicating that they are marked as favorites.
  </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I favorite multiple items at once?">
    Currently, you need to favorite items individually by clicking the "🌟" icon or using the 3-dot menu for each item.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Will favoriting an item affect its tags or highlights?">
    No, favoriting an item does not affect its tags or highlights. All your customizations will remain intact.
    </Accordion>
</Accordions>
