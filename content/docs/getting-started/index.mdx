## <SiAppstore className="inline w-5 h-5 text-[#0D96F6]" /> Folio on App Store (iPhone and iPad)

The Folio app is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. To download and install Folio on your iOS device, you can use one of these two methods:

- Open the App Store and search for `Folio` to find the app.
- Or use this direct link: https://apps.apple.com/eg/app/folio-save-now-read-later/id6746292066

![App Store](/getting-started/download-install-folio/AppStore.png)

## <SiGoogleplay className="inline w-5 h-5 text-[#34A853]" /> Folio on Google Play Store (Android and Tablet)

The Folio app is available on Google Play Store for Android and Tablet devices. You can download it by:

- Searching for `"Folio – Save now. Read later."` in the Google Play Store.
- Downloading directly from here → https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.lessisbetter.folio

![Play Store](/getting-started/download-install-folio/Playstore.png)

## <FaGlobe className="inline w-5 h-5 text-blue-600" /> Folio on the Web

Access Folio through the web version! It's available online and compatible with all browsers ✨.

- Visit the Folio web app directly using [this link](https://app.savewithfolio.com/) → https://app.savewithfolio.com/
- Alternatively, search for `"Save with Folio"` on Google to find the Folio App.

![Folio on the Web](/getting-started/download-install-folio/FolioWeb.png)

## <MdExtension className="inline w-5 h-5 text-purple-600" /> Folio Browser Extensions (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari)

Leverage the power of Folio extensions for easy saving and accessing of your content. Folio extensions are available on: 

- <SiGooglechrome className="inline w-6 h-6" style={{ color: "#2AB6A6" }} /> Chrome  → [Access Chrome Extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/dodpedlanihpjnhfmacllipmamhbkfap?utm_source=item-share-cb)
- <SiFirefoxbrowser className="inline w-6 h-6" style={{ color: "#2AB6A6" }} /> Firefox → [Access Firefox Extension](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/folio-save-now-read-later/)
- <IoLogoEdge className="inline w-6 h-6" style={{ color: "#2AB6A6" }} /> MS Edge → [Access Edge Extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/dodpedlanihpjnhfmacllipmamhbkfap?utm_source=item-share-cb)
- <SiSafari className="inline w-6 h-6" style={{ color: "#2AB6A6" }} /> Safari → [Access Safari Extension](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/save-to-folio/id6749776863)
