## ✉️ How to Sign Up for Folio

Follow these steps to create a new Folio account:

1. Open Folio App (Web, iOS, or Android).
2. Look for the Sign Up screen (it appears by default).
3. Enter your `✉️ Email` address.
4. Create a `🔑 Password`.
5. Confirm your `🔐 Password`.

Finally, click the `Create Account` button to complete your Folio account setup.

![Sign Up](/getting-started/signup-in/SignUp.png)

## 📥 How to Login for Folio

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
  The app saves your login session after you successfully sign in for the first time, so you don't need to sign in every time you access the app.
</Callout>

Signing in is straightforward. Simply enter your `✉️ Email` and `🔑 Password`, then click the `Sign In` button.

![Sign In](/getting-started/signup-in/Login.png)

## 🔐 Forgot Password

To reset your password:
1. Navigate to the Sign In screen.
2. Click the `Forgot Password` option.
3. Enter your registered email address to receive a password reset link.
4. Check your inbox or spam folder for the email.
5. Click on the reset link in the email.
6. Enter and confirm your new password.

![Forget Password](/getting-started/signup-in/Forgot_Password.png)
